  Verb Selection

Distractors come from all 4 verbs (same person).
Tests: do you know which verb fits the context?
Example: SER vs ESTAR · TER vs IR

  Conjugation Mastery

Distractors are other forms of the same verb.
Tests: do you know which conjugation fits the subject?
Example: vou / vais / vai / vamos

Quick reminders
  • SER: permanent — identity, profession, characteristics, origin
  • ESTAR: temporary — states, location, ongoing action (estar a + inf.)
  • TER: possession, physical states (fome/sede/frio), age, obligation
  • IR: movement, near future (ir + infinitive), daily routines
